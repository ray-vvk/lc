<SectionBasic h2="1. Purpose and scope">
  This protocol describes minipreparation of recombinant baculoviral (rBV) DNA from infected Sf9 insect cells. The isolated DNA is intended for research analysis, including verification using appropriate qPCR primers.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="2. Method principle">
  Infected Sf9 cells are collected and subjected to sequential P1, P2, and P3 buffer treatment. Clarified lysate is subjected to isopropanol precipitation and an ethanol wash; recovered DNA is resuspended in TE buffer and evaluated by qPCR. This procedure uses the volumes and centrifugation settings described in Virovek-SOP-002, Rev. 004.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="3. Materials, reagents, and equipment">
  Sf9 cells infected with recombinant baculovirus; ESF AF medium; 6-well plates or culture bottles; microcentrifuge tubes; P1, P2, and P3 miniprep buffers (Qiagen); 99.99% isopropanol; 70% ethanol; TE buffer; CaviCide and 70% isopropanol for work-area cleaning; microcentrifuge; pipettes; and qPCR instrument with appropriate primers. Use laboratory-approved supplies and equipment.

<SectionBasic h2="4. Procedure">
  <h3>4.1. Prepare the work area and collect infected cells</h3>

  1. Clear unnecessary materials and clean the workspace with Metrex CaviCide solution and 70% isopropanol.
  2. Collect 1 mL of baculovirus-infected insect cell culture, or the infected cells from one well of a 6-well plate, into a microcentrifuge tube. Centrifuge at 13,000 rpm for 1 minute.
  3. Discard the supernatant and add 200 µL P1 buffer to the pellet. Vortex to resuspend the cells.

  <h3>4.2. Lyse cells and clarify the lysate</h3>

  1. Add 200 µL warm P2 buffer and invert the tube 10 times to lyse the cells.
  2. Add 200 µL cold P3 buffer, invert 10 times, and incubate on ice for 5 minutes.
  3. Centrifuge at 13,000 rpm for 5 minutes. Transfer the supernatant to a fresh microcentrifuge tube.

  <h3>4.3. Precipitate and wash DNA</h3>

  1. Add 420 µL of 99.99% isopropanol to each tube and invert 10 times to mix.
  2. Centrifuge at 13,000 rpm for 10 minutes and discard the supernatant.
  3. Slowly add 500 µL of 70% ethanol and centrifuge at 13,000 rpm for 5 minutes.
  4. Discard the supernatant and centrifuge again to remove residual liquid.

  <h3>4.4. Resuspend, verify, and store DNA</h3>

  1. Add 30 µL TE buffer and rinse the tube sidewall to recover the DNA.
  2. Perform qPCR using appropriate primers to verify rBV DNA.
  3. Store the recovered baculoviral DNA at −20 °C.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="5. Controls, reporting, and interpretation">
  Record the recombinant baculovirus/sample identifier, infected Sf9 culture source, preparation date, reagent identifiers where relevant, centrifugation settings, final resuspension volume, qPCR primer set, and qPCR results. The source SOP specifies qPCR verification but does not define acceptance criteria, DNA yield targets, or a quantitative purity threshold; these should not be inferred from this protocol.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="6. Laboratory experience and limitations">
  This is an alkaline-lysis-based minipreparation from infected Sf9 cells. Recovery and suitability for downstream applications may vary with culture condition, infection state, and sample handling. Centrifugation is specified in rpm in the source SOP; relative centrifugal force depends on rotor geometry and should be documented when transferring the method to different equipment.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="7. Safety and document control">
  Handle recombinant baculovirus-infected cultures and chemical reagents according to institutional biosafety assessments, chemical safety requirements, and approved waste-disposal procedures. This research protocol is not intended for clinical use or as evidence of GLP compliance.
