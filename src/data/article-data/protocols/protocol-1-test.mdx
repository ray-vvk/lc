<SectionBasic h2="1. Purpose and scope">
  This protocol describes a 96-well reporter-based assay for assessing functional transduction of mammalian cells by AAV vectors. It is applicable to AAV2 and other AAV serotypes. The supported cell lines are HEK293T, HEK293, HepG2, and RC32. Reporter expression is assessed by fluorescence microscopy or an appropriate antigen-specific detection method. Results reflect the specified cell line and assay conditions.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="2. Assay principle">
  Cells are plated one day before AAV exposure. AAV vectors are applied in serum-free DMEM using a five-point, tenfold dilution series. HEK293T, HEK293, and HepG2 assays use 20 µM etoposide as an expression-enhancing condition; RC32 assays use the laboratory-established wild-type adenovirus 5 (wtAd5) helper condition. After overnight exposure, an equal volume of DMEM containing 20% FBS is added, restoring a nominal final FBS concentration of 10%.

  The helper-virus-assisted RC32 assay and etoposide-enhanced assays represent different biological conditions; their readouts should not be interpreted as directly equivalent measures of intrinsic AAV infectivity.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="3. Materials and reagents">
  HEK293T, HEK293, HepG2, or RC32 cells; DMEM; fetal bovine serum (FBS); AAV reporter vector samples; trypsin or validated cell dissociation reagent; tissue-culture-treated 96-well plates; round-bottom 96-well dilution plates; sterile microcentrifuge tubes; single- and multichannel pipettes; fluorescence microscope or equivalent reporter assay equipment. For applicable assays: etoposide stock (20 mM in DMSO) or laboratory-approved wtAd5 helper preparation.
