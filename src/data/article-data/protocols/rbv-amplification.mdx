<SectionBasic h2="1. Purpose and scope">
  This protocol describes the amplification of recombinant baculovirus (rBV) in Sf9 insect cell suspension culture, recovery of the virus-containing culture supernatant, titer determination by qPCR, and preparation for storage. It is intended for laboratory research use.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="2. Method principle">
  A recombinant baculovirus contains a heterologous DNA sequence introduced by recombinant DNA methods. Sf9 insect cells are infected with an rBV stock and maintained in shaking culture to amplify the virus. The clarified supernatant is sampled for qPCR-based titer determination and then prepared for long-term storage. The stated inoculation ratio is a volume ratio, not a defined multiplicity of infection (MOI).
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="3. Materials and reagents">
  Sf9 cell line; recombinant baculovirus stock; ESF AF medium; sodium citrate dihydrate; 70% isopropanol; suitable culture vessels; 250 mL centrifuge tubes; storage bottles and aliquot containers; pipettes; biological safety cabinet; incubator shaker; centrifuge; cell counter; microscope; and qPCR equipment. Use appropriate laboratory-approved equipment and reagent lots.

<SectionBasic h2="4. Procedure">
  <h3>4.1. Prepare the work area and rBV stock</h3>

  1. Prepare the biological safety cabinet according to the laboratory’s established procedures (internal source: Virovek-SOP-001).
  2. Thaw the rBV stock using hand warmth as described in the source SOP; disinfect the outside of the vial with 70% isopropanol before transferring it into the cabinet. Record the rBV identity and stock information.

  <h3>4.2. Prepare and infect Sf9 culture</h3>

  1. Bring the Sf9 culture into the cabinet. Determine cell concentration and viability using the established cell-counting method (internal source: Virovek-SOP-040).
  2. Adjust the Sf9 suspension to 2 × 10⁶ cells/mL with fresh medium.
  3. Add rBV stock at a 1:200 volume ratio (e.g., 1 mL rBV stock to 200 mL Sf9 cell culture). Record inoculum and culture volumes.
  4. Incubate in a shaking incubator at 28 °C, 140 rpm, for 3 days. Record the incubation start and harvest times.

  <h3>4.3. Harvest and determine rBV titer</h3>

  1. Transfer the culture to the biological safety cabinet and dispense into centrifuge tubes.
  2. Centrifuge at 2,000 rpm for 5 minutes. Transfer the clarified supernatant into a fresh bottle in the cabinet.
  3. Remove a 0.1 mL aliquot for qPCR. Hold the remaining supernatant at 4 °C while the rBV titer is determined.
  4. Determine rBV titer by the laboratory’s established qPCR assay (internal source: Virovek-SOP-005). Record the assay result and sample identification. The amplified rBV now can be used to infect insect cells for protein expression, AAV production, or other experimental purposes.

  <h3>4.4. Formulate and store rBV stock</h3>

  1. If long term storage is required, add sodium citrate to a final concentration of 100 mM.
  2. Dispense into appropriately sized, labeled aliquots for the intended research use. Typical vial sizes are 1 mL, 5 mL, or 10 mL.
  3. Store at −80 °C for long-term storage, following the laboratory’s established storage procedure (internal source: Virovek-SOP-037). Stored rBV should be used as seed stock for further amplification, not directly used for protein expression or AAV production.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="5. Controls, reporting, and interpretation">
  Record rBV identity, Sf9 culture identity, initial cell count and viability, culture volume, stock inoculum volume, incubation conditions, harvest date, clarification conditions, qPCR titer and assay reference, sodium citrate formulation, aliquot sizes, and storage location. Retain the qPCR titer file and aliquot record. A qPCR genome-based titer does not, by itself, establish infectious titer.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="6. Laboratory experience and limitations">
  The protocol reflects the conditions documented in Virovek-SOP-003, Rev. 004. The 1:200 inoculation is volume-based and may not provide equivalent infection conditions for rBV stocks of different titers. Centrifugation is specified in rpm in the source SOP; the corresponding relative centrifugal force depends on rotor geometry. The source does not specify shaker speed, minimum cell viability, or a release specification for rBV titer; these should not be inferred.
</SectionBasic>

<SectionBasic h2="7. Safety and document control">
  Handle recombinant baculovirus and infected insect cells under institutional biosafety procedures and approved recombinant DNA practices. Follow applicable laboratory rules for disinfectants, centrifugation, and low-temperature storage. Research use only; not for clinical administration or as evidence of GLP compliance.
